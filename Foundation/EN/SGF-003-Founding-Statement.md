## Founding Statement

ShadowGuard Initiative did not begin with a business plan.

It did not begin with investment capital.

It did not begin with a hunt for market opportunity.

It began with one ordinary human thought:

> **“I wish my father could use the Internet more safely.”**

From that thought came the first practical tool — **Shadvert** — a simple way to check ads, links, and offers that look fine at first glance but may not be.

ShadowGuard Initiative is a commitment to continue in that spirit:

- help people who lack technical training, talent, or the desire to fight jargon;
- translate complex technology into everyday language;
- reduce fear and confusion about threats — without needless drama;
- build on **simplicity, reliability, security, and trust**.

We do not want people to fear the Internet.  
We want them to understand it well enough to make safer decisions.

Every future application, document, and line of code under this initiative should stand up to one question:

> **Will this help someone feel safer online?**

If the answer is yes — we continue.  
If not — we stop and look for a better solution.

---

Great projects often begin with a large budget.

ShadowGuard Initiative began with one simple idea: help a person who matters.
