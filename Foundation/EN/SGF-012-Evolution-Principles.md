## 1. Purpose

These principles say **how ShadowGuard Initiative may and may not change over time**.

Design Principles (SGF-007) govern the screen.  
Trust Principles (SGF-008) govern trust and data.  
**Evolution Principles** govern generation-to-generation change — and what we never trade for speed.

They were sharpened by the **First Creation** milestone (D-019): the first live version is memory, not waste.

---

## 2. Ten evolution principles

### 1. Significant versions are not deleted
Major product generations go into the **Origin Gallery** (archive / demo / show-and-tell), not the trash.

### 2. The user is not a guinea pig
The live default for a real person is never rewritten by a silent experiment.  
Experiments: branch, lab, opt-in, preview.

### 3. New features must earn trust first
Done means a person understands it and feels safer — not only that the build passed.

### 4. Each generation should be better — and remember why it began
Better ≠ more widgets. Better = clearer, safer, calmer, more honest.

### 5. Change is not the goal
Change must serve experience and trust.

### 6. Universal Accessibility — long-lived compatibility
Technology must not needlessly exclude people.

ShadowGuard aims for the **longest practical support of older OSes and devices** (including older Android and modest hardware), unless that conflicts with security.

New features must **not** force a phone or PC upgrade.

> Ending support for a platform requires a **clear security or technical reason**, never a marketing one.

### 7. Respectful Interface — no commercial clutter
The interface belongs to the user.

ShadowGuard will **not** interrupt with pop-up ads, flashing banners, aggressive marketing, third-party promo noise, or spammy beeps.

**The screen is a workspace, not an ad surface.**

*Trust cannot be built by interruption.*

**Narrow exception:** product notices only when they have **direct safety or operational value** (critical fix, token expiry, required security change).  
**Never:** “Buy Premium”, partner promos, third-party ads, visual mess.

### 8. Calm Interface — every element earns its place
If an element does not help understand the situation or decide well, it does not belong on the screen.

### 9. Technology serves people, not hardware
People should not replace devices because of our software. Where possible, **our software adapts to their device** — even a phone that is six or seven years old.

### 10. Think slowly; only then ship clean code
*We write code quickly only when the ideas were written slowly.*  
Large changes go through the Decision Protocol (SGF-009) and the Book of Decisions.

## 6. Pre-flight before a generation ships to people who trust us

1. Better for trust and clarity — not only “more modern”?  
2. Previous generation preserved in Origin Gallery?  
3. Still workable on modest / older devices where security allows?  
4. Any ads or UI clutter? → **stop**.  
5. Decision recorded (D-xxx)?
